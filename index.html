User Manual & App Guide

Comprehensive instructions for all TN-TransitAI features

1. Route Planning (All 38 Districts) Dijkstra Highway Graph
Select your Origin District and Destination District from all 38 Tamil Nadu districts. Choose your date & simulated weather condition (Clear, Rain, Fog, Storm). Click "Run AI Route & Schedule Analysis" to calculate shortest highway distance, transit hubs, and total travel time.
2. AI Arrival Delay Prediction 97.8% ML Accuracy
Our trained Machine Learning model predicts expected highway arrival delays in minutes based on traffic rush hours, weekend holiday surges, weather penalties, and ghat road transit (Ooty/Kodaikanal).
3. Fleet Radar & Real HSRP Number Plates Real Road Fleet
Filter buses by All Fleet, Currently Running on Highway, or 1 to 1 Non-Stop Normal Bus. View authentic commercial HSRP yellow number plates mapped to real RTO divisions (e.g. TN 01 AN 7892, TN 58 N 9123, TN 38 N 6541) and live highway milestones.
4. Live GPS Radar & Moving Bus Monitor Active Highway Cockpit
Click the "Radar" button on any bus to launch the **Live Moving Bus Telemetry Cockpit**. Watch the bus marker actively glide along the highway in real-time with pulsing sonar waves, directional bearing rotation, live speedometer gauge (`km/h`), live ticking GPS coordinates, distance progress bar, and simulation controls (`1x`, `3x`, `8x` and Auto-Follow camera).
5. Ticket Booking & Storage Offline Browser Storage
Click "Book" on any bus. Enter passenger name (auto-filled if logged in). A confirmed ticket with PNR reference (TN-TKT-XXXXXX) is instantly issued and saved safely in your browser's localStorage. View them anytime in the My Tickets tab.
6. 6-Theme Multi-Color Palette Customizer Color Studio
Click the "Change Design" button anytime to select from 6 handcrafted themes: ☀️ Spendorio Sunset Bright (crisp daylight mode), 🌌 Midnight Cosmic Nebula (high-contrast dark mode), 🌿 Emerald Western Ghats (iconic TNSTC green livery), 🌊 Marina Bay Ocean (coastal blue), 👑 Royal Amber Highway (golden sleeper coach), and 🔮 Cyberpunk Synthwave (neon violet & magenta).
7. Automatic Satellite Weather & Road Grip Sensor Auto-Detect & Cockpit Telemetry
Weather is monitored automatically! When you select an origin district or detect your GPS, real-time satellite weather, temperature, humidity, wind velocity, and road surface conditions (dry, wet, fog, hydroplaning hazard) are fetched in real-time. In the GPS Radar Cockpit, Gauge 5 dynamically senses micro-climates as your bus drives through Tamil Nadu highways. You can also click the weather simulation pills (Clear, Fog, Rain, Storm) anytime to simulate storm delays.
